About Us

WHO WE ARE:

In 1983, our founder, John Snow, recognised a vital gap in the technology market. Many businesses and individuals were struggling to find replacement parts for discontinued, rare machinery, or their current machines that parts, you cannot find anymore. To solve this, he established a company dedicated to sourcing and stocking hard-to-find components at an affordable cost. Today, we have expanded from that single idea into a global network with 20 stores worldwide, ensuring your laptop equipment keeps running smoothly and in good/well condition.

OUR MISSION:

Our mission is to provide affordable, genuine parts for machines/objects that customers cannot find anymore. We have all types of parts for laptops that you cannot find anymore such as MacBook M1 parts/ MacAir parts, etc. Even for Laptops like the Google ChromeBooks!!!
